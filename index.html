The Knowledge System

Reading the sky through an Indian lens.

Jyotisha represents a long-standing Indian tradition involving astronomical observation, calendrical calculation and interpretive systems. This project explores those concepts through a modern computational framework.

01

Nakshatra

The traditional division of the zodiac into 27 lunar mansions, each spanning 13°20′.

02

Rashi

The 360° zodiac is divided into twelve Rashis, each occupying 30°.

03

Panchanga

A traditional calendrical framework involving Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga and Karana.

04

Grahas

Traditional Jyotisha works with nine Grahas, including the lunar nodes Rahu and Ketu.
